# Embracing the AI Powered Developer Life

Are programmers becoming irrelevant? Can anybody really launch a SaaS business on Replit? Should I start learning how to mow grass or make lattes?

This month we welcome Andrew Hanson who will share his experiences trying to replace himself with AI and coming to terms with becoming an AI powered developer.

Andrew Hanson is an Independent Software Developer and been creating software for 20+ years.
